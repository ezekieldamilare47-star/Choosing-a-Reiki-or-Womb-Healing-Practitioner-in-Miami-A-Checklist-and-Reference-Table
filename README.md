Reiki is an energy practice that originated in Japan in the early twentieth century. Practitioners work with a light touch or with hands held just above the body, and sessions are typically quiet and unhurried. People commonly seek it for relaxation and a sense of calm.

In Japanese, hara refers to the abdomen, the area below the navel that many traditions treat as the body's centre. Womb Hara Healing takes its name from that idea and places attention on the womb and lower abdomen.
